Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch between monthly and annual plans?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your billing frequency at any time from your account settings. Subscriptions will adjust automatically.

What happens when a diagram is shared?

Under the Free tier, shared diagrams are public and viewable by anyone with the link. Premium and Team tiers allow you to password-protect shared layouts or restrict access to specified workspace members.

How do team user licenses work?

The Team plan includes pro-rated developer licenses. Additional seats can be purchased dynamically at a pro-rated price based on your current billing cycle duration.

Do you offer refunds?

All subscription purchases, upgrades, seat additions, and renewals are strictly final and non-refundable. You can disable auto-renewal in your account settings at any time to prevent future billing. For details, please review our Refund Policy.
