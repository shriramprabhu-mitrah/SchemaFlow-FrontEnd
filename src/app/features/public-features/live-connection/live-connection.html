Why Live Connection?

!

Eliminates Manual Exports

Stop asking your DBAs to run pg_dump or export DDL scripts every time a schema changes. DBNexus connects directly and reads your database's metadata in real-time.

✓

Guarantees Accuracy

Manual documentation quickly becomes outdated. By connecting directly to your live staging or production environment, you ensure your architecture diagrams are 100% accurate.

★

Automates DBML Generation

We automatically translate your live tables, columns, and foreign key relationships into readable DBML code, which you can edit, version control, or use to generate interactive data dictionaries.

Instant Reverse Engineering

Securely connect to your live database to extract tables, columns, and relationships in seconds. Keep your diagrams automatically synced with your production or staging environments.

Secure Encrypted Connections

Your database credentials never leave your browser unencrypted. We prioritize security to ensure your data models and connections are fully protected.

Multiple SQL Flavors

We natively support pulling live schema metadata from PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Microsoft SQL Server right out of the box.

Always Up-to-Date

Forget about manually running DDL export scripts. Our secure live connector reads the information_schema directly to keep everything perfectly synced.

One-Click Schema Sync

Connect instantly and visualize your complex architectures without any configuration overhead. Safe, secure, and blazing fast.
